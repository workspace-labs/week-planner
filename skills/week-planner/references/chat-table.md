# The plan as a chat table

Where code cannot run, hand the plan over in this shape. Keep the same order and words as the PDF would.

```markdown
**This week** · Sun 4 Oct – Sat 10 Oct 2026 · 16 h planned of 18.5 h free
**#1 goal:** Deliver the client report · Why: the client signs off this month · Done when: the client
says yes · Could stop me: the client is slow to reply · First step: draft the report on Monday morning ·
This week: 3 tasks · 6 h · Mon to Thu

| Day | Plan | Hours |
|---|---|---:|
| Sun 4 Oct | 🔴 Choose the new logo (your decision) | 1 of 2 |
| Mon 5 Oct | Draft the client report (active) · Plan the team offsite | 3 of 3 |
| Tue 6 Oct | Send the report for review (active) · Gym | 3 of 3 |
| Wed 7 Oct | Review the new website (review) · 🔴 Approve the budget · Buffer 1 h | 2.5 of 3 |
| ... | ... | ... |

**Waiting on you:** Choose the new logo (Sun) · Approve the budget (Wed)
**Carried over:** Book the dentist appointment

**Advice for your week** (from the AI, nothing here is on your board)
1. **Send the report early.** The report goes out Tuesday, so a slow answer still lands before Thursday.
   *Because you said: "The client is slow to reply"*
```

- 🔴 marks a decision waiting on the person. The chat table is the one place an emoji is fine.
- "Planned" counts buffer time, in the top line and in each day's hours; the score counts only real work.
- In a review, add ✅ to done items and end with **Score: 7 of 10 done**, **Moves to next week:** ... and
  each goal's verdict (Reached, Close or Not yet).
- Then give the plan file as a ```json block, so a later session with code can draw the PDF.
